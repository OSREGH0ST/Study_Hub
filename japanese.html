🔗 Particles (Core Grammar)

がSubject Marker (ga)
をDirect Object (wo)
にTime / Destination (ni)
でPlace of Action / Means (de)
へDirection (e)
とWith / And — Complete list (to)
やAnd — Incomplete list (ya)
のPossession 's (no)
もAlso / Too (mo)
から / までFrom / Until (kara/made)
